# Cyberpunk TCG Art Gallery

An unofficial, fan-run collection of card art and official marketing images for the **Cyberpunk Trading Card Game**, put together by the St. Louis Cyberpunk TCG community.

This is not affiliated with or endorsed by CD PROJEKT RED or WeirdCo.

## What's in here

Every card folder is named **Character / Subtitle** (for example `Adam_Smasher/Ender_of_Legends`). Image files inside are named by printing and card number.

## What's linked instead of uploaded

Artwork the artists posted on ArtStation is **linked, not re-uploaded**, out of respect for the Artists and their agreements with ArtStation. The same goes for regular printing art, studio art and starter deck art. Each card's README lists the links.

What is mirrored here is the card images themselves, photos of pulled cards, and images that were published as official marketing (each with its source noted).

## Removal requests

Any artist can request to have their art removed from the repo. The same applies to any rights holder.

To ask, [open an issue](https://github.com/TwitchyPanda/Cyberpunk-TCG-Art-Gallery/issues/new) naming the card or file. It will be taken down.
